Cervical Cancer Fact Sheet

Understanding the Body spa

The cervix connects the uterus to the birth canal. Cells change very slowly over 5-10 years, making early detection 100% actionable.

Vaccine Protection Rate

1. Signs to Watch For (Early & Intermediate)

Early changes often have no symptoms. When present, pay attention to these normal body signals without panic:

water_drop Unusual Bleeding Bleeding between monthly cycles or after marital relations.
healing Pelvic Discomfort Persistent low pelvic or back pain not tied to your menstrual week.
water Changes in Discharge Persistent watery or colored discharge with a distinct unusual odor.

2. How to Lower Your Risk & Protect Family

vaccines
HPV Vaccination (Ages 9-14) Free at primary schools and health centers across Rwanda. Two simple doses give lifetime protection against high-risk strains.
medical_services
Visual Inspection (VIA) / Pap Smear Recommended every 3 years for women aged 25 to 49. Results are available on the very same day at district health centers.

3. Community Myth vs. Medical Fact

cancel Myth Debunked

"Only older grandmothers get cervical cancer."

A widespread belief that young mothers do not need screening before menopause.

touch_app Tap to view clinical fact
check_circle Verified Fact

It frequently strikes women in their prime reproductive years (30–49).

What to do: Screen regularly every 3 years. Early detection stops cervical abnormalities before they turn invasive.
